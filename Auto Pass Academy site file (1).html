Questions

Before You Book

How do I book a lesson?

Call or text 604-401-2655. Bookings are by phone and appointments are required — there’s no online booking form, so you’ll always confirm your time directly with Navid.

Which package should I choose?

It depends on your starting point. Complete beginners usually do best with Silver (5 × 90 min), Gold (10 × 90 min), or Ultimate (15 × 90 min). If shorter sessions suit your schedule better, Bronze gives you 10 lessons at 60 minutes each for $55 per lesson — the lowest per-lesson price. Still unsure? A single $60 lesson is an easy way to start.

What’s the difference between Bronze and Gold?

Both include 10 lessons. Bronze ($550) lessons are 60 minutes each; Gold ($850) lessons are 90 minutes each, so you get 15 hours of practice instead of 10, with more time per session for highway driving and test routes.

What does the Road-Test Warm-Up include?

For $120, you get a 1-hour warm-up lesson before your ICBC road test to settle your nerves and sharpen the key manoeuvres — and the school car is included for your test, so you take it in the car you’ve practised in. Call or text with your test date to arrange it.

Who are lessons for?

Teens and adults of all ages — complete beginners, drivers preparing for their Class 7 or Class 5 road test, and experienced drivers who want a refresher. Instruction is patient and one-on-one.

Is pick-up and drop-off really free?

Yes — pick-up and drop-off is free with every package, at no extra cost. When you book, confirm your pick-up spot in Coquitlam or the Tri-Cities, and we’ll pick you up for your lesson and drop you off afterwards.

Where do lessons take place?

Auto Pass Academy is based at 1150 Johnson St, Coquitlam, and serves Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam, and Port Moody — the Tri-Cities. With every package, free pick-up and drop-off is included, so you don’t need to come to us first. Call 604-401-2655 to confirm pick-up arrangements for your area.

School Policies

Our Policies

Clear, fair terms for every student — how booking, payment, cancellations, and road test day work at Auto Pass Academy. By booking a lesson or package, you agree to the policies below.

Booking & Appointments

  • All lessons are by appointment only — call or text 604-401-2655 to book.
  • Lesson times are confirmed directly with your instructor when you book.
  • Pick-up and drop-off is free with every package — confirm your pick-up location in Coquitlam or the Tri-Cities when you book, and we’ll pick you up and drop you off at no extra charge.
  • Please arrive on time and ready. If you’re running late, call or text — lessons still end at the scheduled time so the next student isn’t kept waiting.
  • Packages are for one student, are not transferable, and lessons should be used within a reasonable period — confirm your package’s expiry when you book.

Fees & Payment

  • All prices are listed on this page under Packages & Pricing — no hidden fees, and pick-up and drop-off is included free with every package. Prices are in Canadian dollars and may change with notice; the price confirmed at booking is the price you pay.
  • Payment is due before or at the start of your lesson, and packages are paid in full before the first lesson unless another arrangement is confirmed when you book.
  • ICBC fees are separate — road test and licensing fees are paid by the student directly to ICBC and are never included in lesson prices.
  • A receipt for your payment is available on request.

Cancellations & Rescheduling

  • Need to change your lesson? Call or text as early as you can — at least 24 hours ahead — and we’ll reschedule at no charge.
  • Lessons cancelled with less than 24 hours’ notice, and missed lessons (no-shows), are charged in full or deducted from your package — that time was held for you.
  • If we ever have to cancel — illness, vehicle issues, or unsafe weather — your lesson is rescheduled at no charge, or refunded if you prefer.

Refunds

  • Changed your mind about a package? Unused lessons are refundable — lessons already taken are charged at the package’s per-lesson rate shown on this page, and the balance is returned to you.
  • Single lessons and the road-test warm-up are refundable up to 24 hours before the appointment.
  • To request a refund, call or text 604-401-2655. Approved refunds are processed within 10 business days.

Student Requirements

  • You must hold a valid BC learner’s licence or driver’s licence and bring it to every lesson — by law, a lesson cannot take place without it, and the lesson will be treated as a late cancellation.
  • All licence conditions apply during lessons — for example, glasses or contacts if your licence requires them, and the L / N display rules when you’re driving.
  • Students under 19 need a parent or guardian’s consent to enrol.
  • Tell us about anything that could affect your driving — a medical condition, medication, or a licence restriction — so lessons can be planned safely.

Safety & Conduct in the Car

  • Seatbelts are mandatory for everyone in the car, at all times. No exceptions.
  • No phones while driving. Your phone stays put away during the lesson — BC’s distracted-driving laws apply in full.
  • Never attend a lesson under the influence of alcohol, cannabis, or any impairing substance. The lesson will be cancelled and charged in full.
  • The instructor may pause or end a lesson if driving becomes unsafe, and may take control of the vehicle at any time to keep everyone safe.
  • Please treat the instructor and the training vehicle with respect — no food or open drinks in the car.

Road Test Day

  • Booking your ICBC road test is your responsibility — book it with ICBC, then call or text us with the date and time so the $120 warm-up can be lined up.
  • Bring your licence and your ICBC appointment confirmation on test day, and arrive early — ICBC may cancel a test if you arrive late or without the required documents.
  • The $120 warm-up includes a 1-hour warm-up lesson before your test and use of the school car for your ICBC road test — you test in the car you’ve been learning in.
  • We prepare you thoroughly, but no school can guarantee a pass — the result is decided by the ICBC examiner on the day. If you don’t pass, we’ll debrief and plan your next steps together.

Privacy, Photos & Concerns

  • Your name, phone number, and lesson details are used only to book and deliver your lessons. We don’t sell or share your information.
  • Photos or videos of students — for example, a pass-day photo for Instagram — are taken and posted only with your permission.
  • Questions or concerns about a lesson or these policies? Call or text 604-401-2655 and we’ll sort it out with you directly and promptly.
  • Auto Pass Academy operates as an ICBC Approved driving school in British Columbia and follows ICBC’s driver training standards.

Policy updates: These policies may be updated from time to time. The version posted on this page applies to new bookings, and any change that affects an existing package will be discussed with you before it applies. Last updated November 2026.
